## Operational and research limits

The executor is a local state machine with single-writer locks, not a LangGraph/distributed service. A process kill can leave a lock; verify no writer is active before removing a stale lock. Checksums detect corruption, not malicious modification; local checkpoints/factories are trusted application inputs. Adapter timeouts remain adapter-owned. Changes to input/model/budget/prompt identities require a new run ID.

Critic approval is not factual truth, suitability or human approval. Complete critic context exceeding the explicit profile cap (8,000 default; 64,000 expanded) returns review_incomplete. Role prompts remain capped at 8,000. Revision feedback exceeding capacity fails validation without truncation. Selection parameters are experiment inputs, not calibrated defaults.
