# Presenter walkthrough

Suggested duration: approximately seven minutes. This is a rehearsal outline, not a measured presentation duration.

| Step | Action | Explain / expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · 45 seconds | Open the five-company workspace. Point to the synthetic label and fixed cutoff. | “Agent of Odds combines cited research with deterministic selection, constrained maths and simulation. This demo uses invented data and scripted generation.” |
| 2 · 45 seconds | Click **Load demo policy**. Show company checkboxes, cash/asset limits and execution limits. | Inputs are explicit test values. Five/twenty universes are configurable; no actual investor policy is supplied. |
| 3 · 90 seconds | Click **Run fixture research**. Wait for SUCCESS. Expand a fundamental report, news report and **Cited passage**. | Separate roles retain source IDs, dates and version hashes. News interpretation is uncertain. Numerical statistics, covariance and weights come from Python code. |
| 4 · 45 seconds | Show the selection reasons, reviewed allocation and critic rationale. | Eligibility precedes allocation. Hard constraints are checked independently. Critic agreement cannot establish factual truth, suitability or investment merit. |
| 5 · 60 seconds | Review **Paper portfolio**, fill date, fees and exact journal. Check the consent box and click **Approve paper portfolio**. Reload. | Approval is explicit and bound to the proposal. Repeat/reload preserves the receipt. Current approval time differs from historical fill time. This creates an isolated synthetic book. |
| 6 · 45 seconds | Choose a fixture date and click **Update prices only**; save the other date. Select the earlier **Replay saved valuation**. | Shares/cash stay fixed. Valuation is numerical and makes no model calls. Original research cutoff, fill, approval, valuation and recording times remain distinct. |
| 7 · 45 seconds | Open evidence/m5_2_comparison.md; run archive replay in another terminal if desired. | Combined/separate research retain all candidates and match numerical-only in these fixtures. Calls differ; output limits match. Actual inference tokens/cost are unknown. The synthetic holdout tests separation, not financial generalisation. |
| 8 · 45 seconds | Show the twenty-company workspace or audit row; conclude with limitations. | The expanded proof measured local runtime and synthetic coverage. Shared evidence, three sessions and repeated patterns cannot support investment conclusions. The next research step needs independent evidence and richer evaluation. |

## Failure demonstration (optional, 1–2 minutes)

Create a new run after **Load demo policy**, choosing **Rejected review**: rejected output hides allocation and approval controls. Choose **Quota interruption** for another run: explicit retry resumes the saved stage. Choose **Missing evidence**: missing support remains an explicit outcome, not a negative investment opinion. These are controlled fixture scenarios; do not describe them as current provider incidents.

## Fallback if the browser is unavailable

Open the recorded comparison Markdown, then execute archive-only replay. Show the source hashes, frozen decision identities and exact results in JSON. The presenter notes and verification record remain available. Report that the browser demonstration was unavailable rather than claiming a fresh UI run.

## Closing statement

“The verified contribution is a traceable research-to-simulation workflow with bounded generation, explicit constraints, numerical checks and reproducible comparisons. The current synthetic results do not establish superior stock selection or returns.”
